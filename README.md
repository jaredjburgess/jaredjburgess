## Current work

### Aster

**Mission: build a private AI health researcher grounded in your own
longitudinal data.**

Aster is a local-first health intelligence app for macOS that brings
wearable, nutrition, blood-test and genetic data into one coherent,
source-aware view.

Its goal is to turn fragmented health information into a trustworthy
foundation for cited AI analysis and conversation. I am building it end to
end, including the React/TypeScript interface, local data layer, external
integrations and privacy-separated agentic AI pipeline.

Aster is currently in private alpha and its source remains private.
